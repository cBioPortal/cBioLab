Without strata, a gene that marks a cancer type carries that type's prognosis; on a cohort of several cancer types, stratify by CANCER_TYPE. Each patient is scored only by models that did not train on them. The group cuts are equal quantiles of the score, chosen without the outcome. Genes outside the panels every patient was sequenced on are not candidates, so a patient is never counted as wild-type for a gene they were not tested for. The C-index ignores delayed entry. Seeded (seed 1): the same document gives the same model.
