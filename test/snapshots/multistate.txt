Progression is the first Diagnosis event with PROGRESSION in Y / Yes after day 0; death and the end of follow-up are OS_STATUS / OS_MONTHS (OS_MONTHS × 30.4375 days on the timeline's clock; checked: 0 patients of 5,543 with a timeline event more than 31 days past it, a study above 5% is refused). 1,941 patients had a progression on or before day 0 and start in the progression state. Markov, clock-forward: every transition's hazard is a function of time since day 0, progression → death included (a patient enters that risk set at their progression), so time already spent progressed does not enter the model. Rates are events per 100 person-months in the state left (95% interval, Poisson); cumulative hazards Nelson–Aalen (95% interval, log scale); state occupation Aalen–Johansen from the transitions, drawn without intervals while at least 10 of a cohort's patients are followed. Hazard ratios: one Cox model per transition (Efron ties), each cohort against Colorectal, stage 1-3; a cohort with fewer than 5 events in a transition gets none. Progression here is a reading of the record: in MSK-CHORD an NLP model's call on each radiology report, so it is probabilistic and only as dense as the imaging — 41 patients in the model have no PROGRESSION record at all and could never be seen to progress, and their deaths count as death without progression. Left out: 0 without follow-up. The cohorts are not randomised, so differences are associations. Each site sends only (prevalent, progression time, end time, dead) counts.
