Patients altered in at least one of 3 genes per CANCER_TYPE group, over the patients profiled for at least one of them (the union of their panels, as an oncoprint's merged row counts it). Any mutation, high-level copy-number change (amplification, deep deletion) or structural variant counts; low-level gains and losses do not. A patient with more than one kind of alteration across the genes counts once, under "multiple". A patient counts in every group one of their samples in the cohort falls in, profiled if any of those samples is, altered if any is. CANCER_TYPE is read per sample and folded on case and whitespace; samples with no value form their own group. Groups with fewer than 200 profiled patients are pooled into one bar rather than dropped.
