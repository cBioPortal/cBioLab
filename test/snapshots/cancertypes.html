Patients altered in at least one of 3 genes per CANCER_TYPE group, over the patients profiled for at least one of them (the union of their panels, as an oncoprint's merged row counts it). Any mutation, high-level copy-number change (amplification, deep deletion) or structural variant counts; low-level gains and losses do not. A patient with more than one kind of alteration across the genes counts once, under "multiple"; an amplification and a deep deletion are one kind (copy number), shown as the amplification. A patient counts in every group one of their samples in the cohort falls in, profiled if any of those samples is, altered if any is. Grouped by CANCER_TYPE, the portal's default category for a cohort of several cancer types. CANCER_TYPE is read per sample and folded on case and whitespace; a sample without one takes its study's type of cancer, as the portal's. A group is drawn when it has at least 200 profiled patients; the rest are counted in "N of M categories are shown".
