Coverage is read from the mutation panels and calls from the cohort's own samples only; a fusion driver is called only where the study reports structural variants. Then every other gene's mutation frequency is compared between driver-negative and driver-positive patients, in PATIENTS over the patients profiled for that gene (a panel that lists it, or whole-exome; an off-panel call counts as measured), two-sided Fisher's exact test, Benjamini–Hochberg q over the 487 genes tested; 7 altered genes were not tested (fewer than 5 altered patients, or not profiled in a group). The log ratio is log2 of the driver-negative share over the driver-positive one. Driver-positive tumours are often mutually exclusive with other drivers by biology, so a gene enriched in the driver-negative group is a candidate alternative driver, not a proven one; smoking history and tumour mutation burden also differ between the groups and are not adjusted for.
