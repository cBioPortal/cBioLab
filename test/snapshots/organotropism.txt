  CAVEAT (tell the user): Sites are the values of TUMOR_SITE on Diagnosis timeline events, at any date: a patient with any site recorded is evaluable for every site, and has a site when an event names it. The timeline's tumour sites include the primary tumour's own organ, so the column for the organ a cancer type starts in mostly records primaries. Per site and gene, the patients evaluable for the site and profiled for the gene (a mutation panel listing it, or whole-exome; a patient not profiled is in neither group, never wild-type) are split by metastasis there and by a mutation the OncoKB store (v7.5) calls oncogenic or likely oncogenic in the gene called on a covering panel, in any of their samples. The 2×2 tables are stratified by CANCER_TYPE per patient (“Multiple” when samples disagree, a stratum of its own; 1 stratum), and tested by the Cochran–Mantel–Haenszel test (continuity-corrected) with the Mantel–Haenszel common odds ratio — so a gene is compared with metastasis WITHIN cancer types, and a cancer type that both carries the gene and spreads to the site does not make the association by itself. A cell is tested with at least 5 altered patients, 5 with the site and 5 without; Benjamini–Hochberg q over the 60 cells tested. The colour is log2 of the odds ratio. A site is only recorded where imaging, biopsy or the source's own abstraction found it, so detection follows practice (who was scanned, how often, which sites are biopsied); patients sequenced from a metastasis are more likely to have that site recorded. Associations are not adjusted for stage, time followed, treatment or tumour mutation burden. The lower panel: cumulative incidence of the first CNS/Brain event after the timeline's day 0 for patients profiled for EGFR, altered against not, death (OS_STATUS) competing, Aalen–Johansen with delta-method 95% intervals; patients with the site at or before day 0 are excluded rather than counted as free of it (EGFR altered 410, EGFR not altered 942), as are those without follow-up. Censoring at OS_MONTHS on the timeline's clock; 0 patients of 6,457 have the event after the last follow-up (refused above 5%). Gray's test by permutation (2000 shuffles, seeded).
