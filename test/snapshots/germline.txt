  CAVEAT (tell the user): Carriers are patients with a mutation the study marks GERMLINE (mutation status, case-insensitive) in the gene, counted per patient over all their samples and only among the patients sequenced for the gene (a tumour mutation panel listing it, or whole-exome); none fell outside a panel. A study reports germline variants only for the patients it analysed and consented for it, and the warehouse cannot tell a patient tested and negative from one never tested, so the carrier share is of patients sequenced, not of patients germline-tested: a lower bound where germline testing was partial. A somatic second hit in a carrier is another mutation in the same gene that is not germline (any called status but GERMLINE; uncalled and off-panel calls excluded) or a deep deletion (copy number −2, among patients profiled for copy number). Loss of heterozygosity — the commonest second hit — needs allele-specific copy number and is not assessed; a deep deletion in a carrier is rare, since it removes the germline allele too. So the second-hit rate is a floor. Non-carriers' rate is the same somatic definition among the sequenced patients without a germline call; Fisher's exact test of carriers' against non-carriers' rate per gene, Benjamini–Hochberg over the genes of each cohort. Germline calls are not pathogenicity-filtered here: whatever the study deposited as germline is counted.
