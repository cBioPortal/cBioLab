  CAVEAT (tell the user): Plasma is SAMPLE_CLASS = cfDNA/ctDNA or a sample on ACCESS129, tissue SAMPLE_CLASS = Tumor; only the cohort's own samples are classed, so a cohort that selects one kind of sample has none of the other to pair. all patients: 617 patients with plasma, 449 patients with tissue, 449 patients with both (601 plasma and 646 tissue samples); ctDNA detected in 453 of the plasma patients (73.4%). Plasma and tissue are paired by patient whenever they were drawn, not by date — the tumour may have changed between them. Per gene, counting only patients whose plasma AND tissue panels both cover it, any alteration (mutation, amplification or deep deletion, structural variant) in both, tissue only, plasma only, or neither; a patient with several samples of a kind is altered there if any is. Positive percent agreement is both ÷ altered in tissue (tissue the reference), with a 95% Wilson interval; overall concordance is (both + neither) ÷ pairs. The genes are the 15 most altered in the paired patients, not chosen in advance. Clonal-haematopoiesis filter: plasma mutation calls in DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1, PPM1D, JAK2, SF3B1, SRSF2, U2AF1, CBL, GNB1, GNAS, IDH2, or seen in the matched normal (white-cell) sample — at least 2 alternate reads there, at an allele fraction at least 0.3 × the plasma's are left out of every count below (copy-number and structural calls are kept; tissue calls are never filtered). all patients — 83 plasma mutation calls of 2,024 removed (41 by gene, 44 by the normal), in 51 patients; ctDNA detected 453 (73.4%) against 457 (74.1%) unfiltered. The gene rule also removes a tumour's own calls in those genes (GNAS, IDH2, SF3B1 and others can be tumour drivers); the allele-fraction ratio keeps calls whose normal reads are tumour DNA in the white-cell sample (far below the plasma's fraction), and a CH clone at very low fraction in blood can still pass; TP53, ATM and CHEK2 are not on the default list because they are tumour drivers as often as CH genes (Razavi et al. 2019; Bolton et al. 2020). Tissue-only calls remain those of a tumour shedding too little DNA. ctDNA detected means any alteration (mutation, amplification or deep deletion, structural variant) called in some plasma sample, over every gene the plasma panel covers. Survival is OS_MONTHS / OS_STATUS as each study records them, from its own origin, which need not be the plasma draw; when the draw comes after that origin the comparison carries immortal-time bias.
