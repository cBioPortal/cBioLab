Categorical attributes are compared with a Pearson chi-squared over the full value vocabulary; numeric ones with Welch's unequal-variance t-test (2 cohorts) or Welch's ANOVA (3+) computed from (n, Σx, Σx²) per cohort — those totals are ADDITIVE across sites, so the pooled mean and variance are exact from per-site aggregates and no individual value is ever transmitted. Counting unit follows the attribute: patients for patient-level, samples for sample-level. q-values are Benjamini–Hochberg across the 2 tested attributes. Numeric attributes also show median/IQR and a rank-based test alongside the moment-based one — a Mann-Whitney U for 2 cohorts, Kruskal-Wallis for 3+ (neither itself part of the q-value correction) — computed from the attribute's complete value distribution, which is only exact and only requested because this view is confined to a single study.
