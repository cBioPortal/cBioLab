# The `segments` view

cBioPortal's **CN Segments** tab (Results and Study View) and the patient view's genomic overview
segment track. In the browser it is igv.js, as the portal draws it (`app/lab/segments-igv.tsx`, fed
by `igv.ts`): drag to pan, zoom, search a gene or locus, sort by clicking, RefSeq genes beneath. The
server SVG (`figure.ts`, for MCP and shared images) draws the same rows with `lib/chart/` (runner
`run.ts`, queries `sql.ts`). Ported from cbioportal-frontend's `resultsView/cnSegments/CNSegments.tsx`,
`shared/lib/IGVUtils.ts` and igv.js 2.11's `SegTrack`.

**Rows** are the cohort's samples with any segment, in the study's own sample order (internal id; the portal's IGV draws
them in the order its segment fetch returns them, which differs); for one
patient, their samples numbered in collection order by `sampleMarks` (`lib/timeline-tracks.ts`), the
markers the patient's other views draw. `sort: "gained" | "lost"` orders by the sample's mean over the
region (bases-weighted; IGV's click-to-sort weights by the region's length). A sample with segments
but none in the region is a grey row: not measured there, never copy-neutral.

**Colours** are IGV's seg-track defaults (`segmentColor` in `lib/chart/style.ts`): white within ±0.1,
a linear ramp to red at +1.5 and blue at −1.5.

**Where**: the whole genome (1–22, X, Y), a chromosome or stretch (`region`), or a gene (`gene`): its
span in IGV's refGene annotation, 1 kb either side, as igv.js zooms to a symbol. The warehouse (like
cbioportal.org's API) has no gene coordinates — `reference_genome_gene.start/end` are 0 — so they come
from `lib/gene-loci.ts`, generated by `scripts/gen-gene-loci.ts` from the files igv.js loads.

**Limits**: at most `rows` sample rows (default 300, up to 2000); the rest are counted and named as
left out, above the rows and in the caption — the portal instead squeezes every sample into the track
(and refuses the whole genome past 20,000 samples). A figure past 250,000 segments is refused, naming
the limit. The result carries each drawn sample's segments over the whole genome, so IGV can pan and zoom
anywhere; `gene`/`region` is only where it starts (and what `sort` and the SVG figure use).

The rows name samples, so both queries run only through `PatientLevelResolver.viewRows` (`need:
"patients"`); neither builder is an aggregate, and neither is named `*Sql`.

**The patient page's genomic overview** (`overview: true`, `app/lab/genomic-overview.tsx`) is the
portal's patient-view IGV (cbioportal-frontend `patientView/genomicOverview/`): the result also carries
the samples' called mutations and each sample's gene panels. The browser draws a MUT track (coloured by
protein impact, `lollipopColor`) above the CNA track, 18 px a sample, with the sample's numbered marker
on the left and its mutation count, altered-genome share and gene-panel badge on the right; compact
(no navigation, ideogram, sequence or RefSeq; half-height ruler; no track menus' sort) until zoomed,
then the full browser, and "Reset view" back. A row clicked in the patient's alteration tables zooms
it to the gene (`app/lab/locus-bus.ts`). The rules it copies are in `igv.ts`, each citing its source.
