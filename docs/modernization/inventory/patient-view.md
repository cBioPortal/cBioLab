## Preface: this whole area is patient-level data

Every feature below shows data about one named patient (or one named sample of a patient): identifiers,
clinical attributes, every mutation, copy-number call, structural variant and timeline event with its
sample, allele counts and dates, plus the pointer to that patient's pathology reports, slides and
files. **Nothing in the Patient View is aggregate-only.** In cBioLab terms the whole page is the
`PatientLevelResolver` side of the line drawn in
[`/home/user/cBioLab/docs/DATA-BOUNDARY.md`](/home/user/cBioLab/docs/DATA-BOUNDARY.md): patient ids may
be read only by a participant entitled to them, and an aggregate-only or remote participant must
neither emit nor receive them. Consequences for the reimplementation:

1. The patient-level views in cBioLab (`timeline`, `alterations`, and the per-patient parts of `plot`/`oncoprint`) already run only through `PatientLevelResolver`, and over a federation peer only where the peer's `FEDERATION_DISCLOSE_PATIENTS` rule and the coordinator's `FEDERATION_ACCEPT_PATIENT_LEVEL` rule both allow it (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, "Patient views"). Any new PV feature that names a patient must follow that route; none may become an `AggregateResolver` method.
2. The portal page also sends patient ids to third parties: the patient's id and samples go to cBioPortal's own API only, but **MRN-keyed trial matching** (`mrn: patientId` to the MatchMiner proxy), **Darwin** (`proxy/checkDarwinAccess` with sample ids), **Digital Slide Archive** (TCGA patient id), the **MSK slide viewer** (slide ids plus a token), the **Google Forms feedback iframe** (current page URL, which carries the patient id, plus the user name) and the **Google Docs PDF viewer** (report URL) all receive patient-keyed values. Variant-level annotation calls (OncoKB, Genome Nexus, CIViC) are keyed by gene/variant/genomic location and tumour type, not by patient id, but the batch itself is the patient's variant list. These are marked `3rd-party` in Notes; each needs an explicit decision in the rebuild.
3. The "cohort" columns in the tables (mutation frequency, CNA frequency, GISTIC, mRNA percentile, reference cohort for the mRNA/Plots tabs) are the only cohort-level (aggregate) numbers on the page; they are fetched as aggregates, but they are rendered beside patient rows, so the denominator rules in cBioLab invariant 7 apply (a frequency is altered / samples profiled for that gene).

**Legend.** E2E file names are relative to `end-to-end-test-playwright/tests/`; `local/` = the dockerized local-DB lane. "Server config" names are `getServerConfig()` keys (`portal.properties`). cBioLab column: `none` = no equivalent, `?` = unsure.

## Open questions

1. **Dead or unreachable code.** `PatientViewPageStore.urlValidationError` is never assigned in `src/pages/patientView`, the IGV "advanced" toggle button is commented out, and `ClinicalAttributesInline.tsx` is entirely commented out. Do we port the validation alert and advanced IGV at all, or drop them? (affects PV-009, PV-035)
2. **Patient-level data sent to third parties.** Trial matching (`mrn` = patient id), Darwin, Digital Slide Archive, the MSK slide viewer, Google Forms (page URL) and the Google Docs PDF viewer all receive patient-keyed values. Under DATA-BOUNDARY.md which of these may a cBioLab patient view call, and must they be disclosed per peer and study like `alterations` is? (PV-018, PV-073 to PV-077, PV-100)
3. **Genomic Evolution state in the URL.** Only seven toggles are in the URL; Group by, sequential mode and the Line Chart/Heatmap sub-tab are not (the sub-tab is `localStorage`). Per invariant 9 these change what a reader sees: should the cBioLab view put them in the document, and what are the agreed defaults (`vaf_sequential_mode_default`, `vaf_log_scale_default`, `oncoprint_clustered_default`)? (PV-058 to PV-063)
4. **Denominators of the Cohort columns.** The mutation "Cohort" (variant counts) and CNA "Cohort" (copy-number counts + GISTIC) columns come from internal endpoints whose panel awareness I did not read. Are they altered / samples profiled for that gene (invariant 7), or a study total? This decides whether cBioLab's `alterations` `freq` is equivalent. (PV-050, PV-095)
5. **Which of the MSK/instance-specific features are in scope.** Trial match, MSK slide viewer, Darwin, GENIE BPC and MSK toxicity timeline colours, the hard-coded HTAN OHSU timeline link, the feature-flagged mRNA and Plots tabs (including hard-coded ADC gene lists), and the per-instance `skin_*` column defaults. Should the modernization carry them, replace them with generic mechanisms (resources, custom tabs, document-level column lists), or drop them? (PV-031, PV-032, PV-075 to PV-077, PV-083 to PV-088)
